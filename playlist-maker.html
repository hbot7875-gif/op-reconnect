How it works

Playlists you add sit at the top of that district's Vault, above the automatically matched ones. Agents see the list and open playlists in Spotify themselves; nothing here plays music. The title and artwork come from Spotify. Each playlist can only be in the Vault once, and only districts with goal songs are listed.
