Privacy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

OP: ReConnect is an independent BTS fan project created and managed by HopeTrackers. This page explains what the game stores, why, and how long it stays.

What we collect

When you create an Agent file — a handle, an email address and a password. Your password is hashed before storage; we never hold the original. You are issued an Agent number, which is what you sign in with.

While you play — your codename, XP, districts, missions, badges, streak and charge, and the other records that make up your progress. A session token is kept in your browser so you stay signed in.

Your streaming activity — if you connect ListenBrainz, Musicat or stats.fm, or scrobble directly, we fetch and store the plays needed to work out your progress: track, artist and when it happened. We store your username for that service so we can look you up. We never receive your password for it.

Vote and event proof screenshots — the image is read by text recognition inside your browser before anything is sent, then uploaded to private storage along with the text that was read and a fingerprint of the image. The fingerprint stops the same screenshot being submitted twice.

Voting proofs — for voting events such as MAMA 2026, a vote certificate is re-saved in your browser before upload, which removes location and other photo data. It is then stored privately with fingerprints of the image. We keep only the results of what we check — that it is a Fans’ Choice certificate, its date, and whether the BTS artist image appears (compared with the official image; no face recognition) — not the text read from the certificate, and not account names, emails or certificate numbers. Reviewers see the image through a link that expires after 10 minutes.

X vote posts — we store the link to the post and the X account that posted it. If you add your X handle, it is used only to confirm that submitted vote posts belong to you and is never shown to other agents. We check public posts through X’s public embed service; we never ask for your X password or connect to your X account.

Badge artwork — artwork uploaded for badges is stored in a public location, so anyone with the link can view it.

Limited game-usage records — we keep a small first-party record of how the game is used: opening the app, opening a district, viewing a screen, building a queue, and which MAMA Battle share options you use (never the text you type on a poster). Each is tied to your Agent number and a random id that lasts only for that browser tab. We use it to understand which parts of the game people actually use.

MAMA Battle Team and Chat — your teammates see your codename and whether you have a verified vote today. If you choose, they also see your Instagram username, with a link to your profile, in Team only. It’s off unless you switch it on, applies to this event only, and you can switch it off at any time. The other team never sees your Team details. Messages you send in Chat, and your reactions, are seen by both teams with your codename and team. If you report a message we keep your report and its reason; the person you reported isn’t told who you are. We also note when you last had Team or Chat open, only to show how many people are online.

Keeping things fair — we record sign-in and recovery attempts to limit abuse, and compare streaming usernames between accounts to spot duplicates.

Inactivity warnings — we record that a warning was sent and whether our email provider accepted it, so an account is never deleted without a warning having gone out first.

What we don't do

We use no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services. The game-usage records above are our own, stay with us, and are never shared or sold. We set no cookies at all. We don't store your IP address, browser user agent, device details or location. We don't sell your data or use it to train AI models. There's nothing to pay for, so we never see payment details.

What your browser stores

Your sign-in details and preferences live in your browser's own storage, not in cookies. Clearing your browser data signs you out and resets preferences.

Other services these pages load

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and embed YouTube (in privacy-enhanced mode) and Spotify players. When those load, those companies receive your IP address and can see you visited that page. We don't control what they do with it and we receive nothing back about you.

The game runs on hosting and email services that keep their own operational logs under their own policies.

How long we keep things

If you retire your Agent file — your personal data is permanently deleted. Proof screenshots are queued for removal from storage and deleted automatically, usually within the hour rather than instantly.

If your Agent file goes quiet — after 14 days of inactivity it's deleted, but only once a warning email has been accepted for delivery by our email provider and you've had time to come back. Time on approved leave doesn't count towards the 14 days.

Event proof screenshots — deleted 30 days after the event they belong to ends.

MAMA Battle Chat — messages, reactions, reports and mutes are deleted 30 days after the event ends, and your Instagram choice is cleared at the same time.

Deletion records — when an account is deleted we keep a short record of what happened. Identifying details in it are removed after 30 days.

Shared content — badge artwork you uploaded stays if other players wear badges made from it, with the link to you removed. Missions you created stay so your teammates keep their history, with your name removed.

Being honest about deletion

“Deleted” isn't the same as “gone from everywhere at once”. Files are removed on an hourly cycle. Encrypted database backups are kept for up to seven days for disaster recovery and aren't edited, so data can survive there until those age out. Service providers may hold their own logs on their own schedules. What we can tell you plainly: nothing identifying is left in the running game, and we never go back to those copies to rebuild anything.

Children

ReConnect does not ask for your age or date of birth, and has no age-verification system. If you're a parent or guardian and believe a child's information is held here, email us and we'll delete the account.

Your choices

You can retire your Agent file at any time from Settings, which deletes your data. You can also email us to ask what we hold, to correct something, to have it deleted, or to raise any privacy concern:

hopetrackervault@gmail.com

Please include your Agent number so we can find the right account.
