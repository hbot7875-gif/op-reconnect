You keep ownership of what you upload. By uploading, you give us permission to store and display it inside the game.

If you believe something here infringes your copyright, see Copyright Credits. We will take it down.

5. Connected streaming services

You can connect ListenBrainz, stats.fm or Musicat, or scrobble directly to ReConnect. For connected services we read your listening history through their public interfaces to count your BTS streams. We never receive your password for those services. You can change or disconnect your source at any time in Settings.

Stream counts come from services we do not control and may be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Your progress depends on data we cannot guarantee.

6. Inactivity

OP: ReConnect deletes inactive agent files. If you do not play for 14 days, your agent file and its data are permanently deleted. Before that happens we send a warning email to the address on your file, and we don’t delete an account unless our email provider accepted that message for delivery. We can’t guarantee it reaches your inbox, so it’s worth keeping your address current.

Time on approved leave does not count towards those 14 days. If you need a break, request leave in-game and the clock pauses.

This is part of how the game works rather than a penalty — the city only stays lit if agents keep streaming.

7. Leaving

You can retire your agent file at any time from Settings. It asks for your handle and your password, because it cannot be undone.

Retiring permanently deletes your agent file and everything in it — your XP, level, badges, districts, streaming history, messages and uploads. We cannot restore it. Uploaded files are removed from storage on an hourly cycle, so they go shortly after rather than the same second.

Three things do not go, and you should know before you decide:

  • Badge photos you uploaded stay in circulation, because other agents wear badges made from them. They stop being linked to you.
  • Missions you created stay, so the teammates who joined them keep their history. Your name is removed from them.
  • We keep a short record that the file was deleted, which is itself deleted after 30 days. Privacy says what is in it.

Deleted data can also persist for up to seven days in our daily database backups, before the backup holding it is rotated out. We do not use backups to bring a retired file back.
