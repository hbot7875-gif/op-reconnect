About

OP: ReConnect is an independent BTS fan project created and managed by HopeTrackers.

It's a streaming game. You get an Agent file, a codename, and a city that's gone dark. Streaming BTS is what lights it back up — your plays become progress, districts get restored, missions get completed, and badges get earned along the way. Connect a streaming source and it keeps count for you, or scrobble directly if you'd rather.

It's made by fans, for fans, and it's free. Nothing to buy, nothing to subscribe to.
