Leaves

Leave pauses the 14-day cleanup clock. Only the agent can start or end a leave.

How leave works
Length
3 to 14 days, starting the moment the agent takes it. Leave can't be scheduled ahead.
Frequency
One at a time. The next can start 14 days after the previous one ends.
Cleanup clock
Leave time is subtracted from the 14 days since the last Bomb charge or verified vote.
Deletion
Still 14+ days after leave, and for agents with an email, a warning delivered 2 to 45 days earlier.
District
The active attempt's deadline moves by the leave length. Streams on leave days don't count toward it.
ARMY Bomb
The charge, or the blackout clock, moves forward by the leave length.
Streak
Leave days bridge the streak without adding to it.
ReConnect
Team quests are not paused; their clocks belong to teammates too.
Ending early
Only the unused part of each change is reversed.

Goals

Goals are per district. Nothing counts toward a district until a goal is assigned.

Each district restores on only the goals assigned to it. A district with no track goal can't be started. Editing a goal only affects districts started after the edit; a district in progress keeps the goals it started with, and every district gets one week from activation. Deleting a goal is safe even if it's live, for the same reason.
